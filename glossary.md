# Project Glossary

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Order Batch** | A collection of pending delivery orders grouped together by a dispatcher for simultaneous route optimization[cite: 10, 11]. |
| **Delivery Route** | An ordered sequence of delivery stops assigned to a specific driver and vehicle for execution[cite: 10, 11]. |
| **Delivery Stop** | A specific destination location on a route where an order must be dropped off within a defined customer time window[cite: 10, 11]. |
| **Payload Capacity** | The maximum total parcel mass (kg) and volume (m³) a vehicle is legally and mechanically allowed to carry[cite: 10, 11]. |
| **Distance Matrix** | A matrix of travel distances and estimated travel times between all stop coordinates, provided by an external mapping API[cite: 11, 12, 17]. |
| **Dispatch Lock** | Optimistic concurrency control mechanism preventing two dispatchers from assigning the same driver or vehicle simultaneously[cite: 9, 12]. |
