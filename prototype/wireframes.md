## 1. Overview & Perspective Coverage
This document presents the low-fidelity wireframe layouts and task flows designed for two key project user roles in the Logistics Route Decider System:
1. **Role 1: Logistics Dispatcher / Fleet Manager (Desktop Perspective)** – Task: Evaluate route optimization alternatives, view constraint parameters, and select/override active transport routes.
2. **Role 2: Commercial Driver / Field Operator (Mobile Perspective)** – Task: Receive clear turn-by-turn guidance, review vehicle constraint warnings, and report live road obstructions or delays.
